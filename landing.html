Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know to get started

What's the difference between private tutoring and group courses?
Private tutoring offers one-on-one attention with personalized curriculum and flexible scheduling. Group courses provide peer interaction, cost savings, and structured progression through levels. Both are excellent options depending on your learning style and goals.
How long does it take to become fluent in English?
The timeline varies based on your starting level and commitment. Most students see significant improvement within 3-6 months of regular study. Complete fluency typically takes 12-18 months with consistent practice and our structured approach.
Do you offer online classes?
Yes! We offer both in-person classes in Mexico City and online classes via Zoom/Google Meet. Online classes maintain the same quality and small group size (max 8 students) as our in-person offerings.
What if I need to cancel or reschedule a class?
We understand life happens! We offer flexible rescheduling options with 24-hour notice. For private tutoring, you can reschedule easily. Group classes offer makeup sessions for missed classes.
Do you prepare students for English exams like IELTS or TOEFL?
Absolutely! We have specialized exam preparation courses for IELTS, TOEFL, Cambridge exams, and more. Our certified teachers use proven strategies to help you achieve your target scores.
